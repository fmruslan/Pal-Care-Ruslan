# Your next 30 days — owner-approved Care refinement

## Customer outcome and scope

The owner approved the attached horizontal calendar mockup as visual direction: **Your next 30 days**, below the unified search/Ask North field and above the unchanged two-column Care cards. The mockup's November dates/product events are not evidence or new data authority. This is capability C/D on the accepted Care/native Insights foundations, not an architecture replacement.

- Use the installed, pinned AG Studio 3.0.0 data engine and supported React custom-widget registration, under the **existing** presentation/license gates. Keep vendor notices. The timeline may use HTML/CSS for its custom rendering, but must actually query Studio and validate returned saved event identities. No decorative SVG presented as native integration.
- Rolling **UTC today plus 29 days**, exclusive upper bound at the next day; show today, five week groups, continuous axis/milestone dots, original product artwork, human event type and explicit date. Mobile/enlarged text scrolls within the timeline rather than overflowing the page. Refresh the server projection at UTC midnight or when returning after a date change.
- Derive events from the **same saved ownership facts as Care and North**, including dated return/price-window deadlines, warranty expirations, delivery estimates/updates and genuinely saved care reminders. No inferred purchase-plus-duration deadlines, shifting old Demo dates into the future, fake maintenance reminders or mockup-specific evidence.
- Keep Demo, verified Sandbox event evidence and recorded-but-unverified counts separate. A verified payment does not verify delivery/warranty/aftercare. Missing/conflicting dates and unsupported observations are not calendar events. Duplicate fact/history references collapse by exact Twin/topic/kind/date; grouped product cards never merge independent dated Twins.
- Each event opens the exact `/vault/<Twin>?milestone=<topic>&careEvent=<fact>` and its corresponding evidence. The optional event selector resolves only from that server-loaded Twin's facts/history; it cannot supply new evidence or authority. **View all care events** opens `/insights?view=care-events#care-events`: the same Studio dataset with a custom timeline, complete dated links, native grid and engine-backed provenance filters. Existing native customer Insights views and saved layouts remain unchanged. Quiet days may be compressed, explicitly disclosed, to keep same-day cards readable on one floating row; the line is continuous, not a claim of uniform elapsed-time spacing.
- Extend the existing model-first North schema with bounded `aftercare/upcoming-care`; compute dates/counts/links with the same server projection. Do not replace ordinary-language interpretation with a rigid keyword parser. Preserve conversations, original answers, owned followup scopes, History and no-replay behavior.
