## Implemented

The customer-first increment reuses the existing monolith, AG Studio 3.0.0 and bounded CareOS/accounting. Customer navigation is Home / Purchases / Insights / Documents. `/insights` offers My Purchases, Documents & Care and Price Adjustments over fixed-scope saved records, with native grid/chart/KPI, custom source/attention/adjustment widgets, cross-filters and validated browser-local layouts. Customer plans have separate conversation ownership and presentation-only commands. Interrupted requests retain their identity; GET recovery never redispatches inference or automatically applies a saved view.
